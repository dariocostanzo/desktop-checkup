# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. We are committed to a welcoming and respectful community for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps create a positive environment include being considerate, welcoming different points of view, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently exclude a contributor.

## Scope and reporting

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public. If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, report it privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include enough information to understand what happened, and do not publish anyone's private information.

Maintainers will review reports as fairly and promptly as possible.
